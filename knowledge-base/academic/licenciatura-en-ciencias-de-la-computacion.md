## Program Overview

The Bacharelado em Ciência da Computação is the flagship five-year undergraduate degree of UDA's Faculdade de Engenharia. Graduates are trained to design, build, evaluate, and lead software systems, data-processing architectures, and information-security systems, as well as to teach and conduct research in Computer Science.

Within the same academic path, the Faculdade also offers a shorter, three-year intermediate degree — the **Técnico em Programação** (University Programming Technician) — which students can complete on the way to the full Bacharelado.

## Competencies

The program builds three broad categories of competency:

### Social and Attitudinal Competencies

1. Perform effectively in work teams, with the ability to organize and lead them.
2. Communicate effectively, both orally and in writing.
3. Act with ethics, professional responsibility, and social and environmental commitment.
4. Learn continuously and autonomously, with the ability to plan that learning.
5. Interpret the evolution of Computer Science with a view toward future technological trends.

### Technological Competencies

1. Identify, formulate, and solve Computer Science problems.
2. Conceive, design, and develop Computer Science projects.
3. Manage, plan, execute, and control Computer Science projects.
4. Effectively use the techniques and tools applicable to Computer Science.
5. Identify and manage risks in Computer Science (environmental, occupational, security, and economic).

### Specific Competencies for the Licenciatura

1. Plan, design, implement, verify, and maintain data-processing system architectures, incorporating emerging aspects of technological change.
2. Plan, design, verify, and maintain communications networks linking data-processing systems, including convergent and software-defined networks.
3. Survey real-world problems, formally specify them, and design, implement, test, and quality-control software and information systems.
4. Specify, design, implement, and maintain information-security components embedded in physical systems and application software.
5. Carry out basic and applied scientific research in Computer Science, with the aptitude to lead projects and direct R&D units and labs.

## Related Intermediate Degree: University Programming Technician (Técnico em Programação)

At UDA's Faculdade de Engenharia, the Bacharelado track includes a shorter, three-year intermediate degree — the Técnico em Programação — that students can complete on the way to the full Bacharelado.

- **Origin and plans of study:** Created in 2004 as a short-duration degree enabling quick entry into the labor market. The curriculum was revised in 2015.
- **Objective:** Train graduates with a basic grounding in the fundamentals of the discipline and in current technologies, qualifying them for professional work on small- and medium-complexity systems.
- **Program Director:** Prof. Rodrigo Fontes

### Professional Scope and Competencies

1. Participate in the survey and analysis of an organization's functional processes, to design associated Information Systems.
2. Participate in the design, implementation, and maintenance of software systems and their databases.
3. Participate in technical-economic feasibility studies related to information-processing systems.
4. Participate as an assistant on Computer Science R&D teams.
5. Evaluate the use, efficiency, and reliability of equipment, software systems, and data existing in companies and organizations.
