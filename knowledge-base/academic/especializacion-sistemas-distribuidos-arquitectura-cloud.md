## Program Overview

The Especialização em Sistemas Distribuídos e Arquitetura Cloud (Specialization in Distributed Systems and Cloud Architecture) is a postgraduate program run by UDA's Faculdade de Engenharia, aimed specifically at university graduates in Computer Science and related Engineering fields.

## Objective

The specialization aims to train professionals with up-to-date knowledge to analyze and develop methods, algorithms, and systems related to distributed computing, cloud-native architecture, fault tolerance, and large-scale system reliability.

In particular, the program covers the theoretical foundations of consensus and replication, alongside the practical engineering tradeoffs of running distributed systems in production. A final capstone project, together with complementary lab work, allows students to integrate and consolidate the subjects studied throughout the specialization.

## Accreditation

The Especialização em Sistemas Distribuídos e Arquitetura Cloud is accredited by Brazil's Ministério da Educação (MEC), Portaria No. 118/16, and holds National Validity granted by the Ministry of Education.
