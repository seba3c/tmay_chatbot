# Volunteering for on-call rotation
## Solvex Pay - Senior Software Engineer, Money Movement Team

### Context
Our on-call rotation had an uneven distribution — a couple of teammates, including Noor, kept getting scheduled for back-to-back shifts because of a scheduling-tool bug, while others rarely rotated in at all. Rather than just flagging the bug and waiting, I volunteered to cover an extra shift immediately while the fix was pending.

### Stakeholders
- Noor and other overloaded teammates
- Money Movement Team (shared rotation)
- Engineering Manager (owned the on-call tooling)

### Table of content
Noticed an uneven on-call rotation caused by a scheduling-tool bug, volunteered for an extra shift to relieve an overloaded teammate immediately, and reported the underlying bug so it wouldn't keep happening.

### Actions
1. Noticed the pattern by checking the rotation history, not just anecdotally — Noor had covered three of the last five weekend shifts.
2. Volunteered to take the next shift myself instead of waiting for the tooling fix, to give Noor an immediate break.
3. Reported the scheduling-tool bug to the engineering manager with the specific rotation history as evidence.
4. Suggested a simple manual audit step before each rotation cycle as a stopgap until the tool was fixed.

### Result and impact
- Noor got an immediate break from the overloaded rotation without waiting on a tooling fix.
- The scheduling bug was fixed within two weeks once it was reported with concrete evidence.
- The manual audit step caught one more scheduling error before the fix shipped.

### Learnings
- Volunteering to absorb an immediate problem while the root cause gets fixed is often more valuable than just reporting the root cause and waiting.
- Bringing concrete evidence (the actual rotation history), not just a general complaint, gets tooling bugs fixed faster.

### Signal areas
- **Ownership**: Took direct action to relieve a teammate rather than waiting for someone else to fix the tooling.
- **Perseverance**: Covered the stopgap shift while also pushing the actual fix through.
- **Communication**: Reported the issue with concrete evidence rather than a vague complaint.
